About PIN code 713215

PIN code 713215 belongs to Paschim Bardhaman, West Bengal. It is used by 2 post offices: Angadpur, Durgapur Brick Field. 1 office delivers post to homes: Angadpur. The others only serve customers at the counter.

What the digits of 713215 mean

  • 7: the postal zone, which covers West Bengal, Odisha, Sikkim, the north-eastern states and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.
  • 71: the first two digits together name the sub-zone, here the West Bengal Circle.
  • 713: the first three digits name the sorting district, where post for this area is sorted.
  • 215: the last three digits name the delivery post office within that sorting district.

Writing this address
