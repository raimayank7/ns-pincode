About PIN code 743375

PIN code 743375 belongs to 24 Paraganas South, West Bengal. It is used by 11 post offices: Usthi, Baneswarpur, Boinchberia, Borijpur and others. Every one of them delivers post to homes.

What the digits of 743375 mean

  • 7: the postal zone, which covers West Bengal, Odisha, Sikkim, the north-eastern states and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.
  • 74: the first two digits together name the sub-zone, here the West Bengal Circle.
  • 743: the first three digits name the sorting district, where post for this area is sorted.
  • 375: the last three digits name the delivery post office within that sorting district.

Writing this address
