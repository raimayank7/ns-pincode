About PIN code 741163

PIN code 741163 belongs to Nadia, West Bengal. It is used by 5 post offices: Betai, Chhitkadaspara, Haripur Chhitka, Karuigachi and others. Every one of them delivers post to homes.

What the digits of 741163 mean

  • 7: the postal zone, which covers West Bengal, Odisha, Sikkim, the north-eastern states and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.
  • 74: the first two digits together name the sub-zone, here the West Bengal Circle.
  • 741: the first three digits name the sorting district, where post for this area is sorted.
  • 163: the last three digits name the delivery post office within that sorting district.

Writing this address
