Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Short version

  • There is no login and no account.
  • What you type in a search box is used inside your browser to find the answer. It is not sent to us and not stored by us.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect any.

What your browser keeps

The site remembers your light or dark choice and, where a page says so, a few recent searches, in your own browser storage. You can clear them any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Visitor counting

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It uses no cookies and does not track you across sites.

Other services this site loads

  • Google Fonts, for the lettering.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the pages and, like any web host, sees your IP address when it serves them.
  • One sponsored card on most pages, loaded from share.groupchaton.com. It is a plain link and picture. If you tap it you go to the sponsor's site, which has its own privacy policy. There are no advertising networks and no advertising cookies on this site.

Children

The site holds reference information only and asks nobody for personal details.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new text and date appear on this page. Questions: contact us.
